I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences (AOS) Program at Princeton University. I have been advised by the late Prof. Larry Horowitz (Princeton AOS / NOAA GFDL), Dr. Meiyun Lin (NOAA GFDL), Dr. Vaishali Naik (NOAA GFDL), and Prof. Stephan Fueglistaler (Princeton AOS). My research interests include atmospheric chemistry and air pollution, with a particular focus on tropospheric ozone, long-range transport, and the impacts of anthropogenic emissions and climate variability on air quality.

I received dual Bachelor of Science degrees in Atmospheric Sciences from the University of Reading (UoR, UK) and Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST, China), graduating with First-Class Honours. During my undergraduate studies, I was advised by Prof. Jian Cao (NUIST) and Dr. Xiaomeng Shen (UoR). I was also a visiting student in the Atmospheric Composition and Air Quality Group at University College London (UCL) during the summer of 2022, under the supervision of Prof. Eloise Marais.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing table tennis, tennis, and chess.
